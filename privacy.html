Privacy policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This policy explains what the Gridoo Reminders add-on for Google Sheets ("the add-on") and this website do with your information. Gridoo ("we") is an independent developer. Contact: gridoo.apps@gmail.com.

The short version

  • The add-on runs entirely inside your own Google account. We have no servers and no database, and your spreadsheet data never reaches us.
  • We never sell, rent or share your data, and we never use it for advertising.

What the add-on accesses, and why

  • The spreadsheet you set it up in. It reads the tab and columns you choose (date, name, days before, recipients) to find reminders that are due. It does not change your cells.
  • Sending email as you. Reminder emails are sent from your own Google account to the addresses you list.
  • Running when you're not present. A daily timer lets reminders go out while the sheet is closed.
  • Your email address. Used only to pre-fill "Send reminders to" and to tell you if a reminder check fails.
  • A sidebar. Shows the settings screen inside Google Sheets.
  • An external service (Pro only). If you enter a licence key, the key alone is sent to our payment provider, Lemon Squeezy, to check that it is valid. No spreadsheet data is sent.

Where your data is stored

Your settings and a list of reminders already sent are saved in the spreadsheet's own properties, inside your Google account. Removing the add-on, deleting its timer (Extensions > Gridoo Reminders > Turn off reminders) or deleting the spreadsheet stops all processing.

Google user data

Gridoo Reminders' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data to develop, improve or train general artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Payments

Pro subscriptions are sold by Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the merchant of record and processes your payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive your name, email and order status from Lemon Squeezy, and use them only to provide and support your licence.

This website and the waitlist

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics. If you join the waitlist, the answers you give in our Google Form are stored in our Google account and used only to tell you about Gridoo add-ons. To be removed, email us and we will delete your entry.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing gridoo.apps@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for important changes, notify Pro customers by email.
