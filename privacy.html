📅 Gridoo Reminders

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This policy explains what the Gridoo Reminders add-on for Google Sheets ("the add-on") and this website do with your information. Gridoo ("we") is an independent developer. Contact: gridoo.apps@gmail.com.

The short version

  • The add-on runs entirely inside your own Google account. We have no servers and no database, and your spreadsheet data never reaches us.
  • We never sell, rent or share your data, and we never use it for advertising.

What the add-on accesses, and why

  • The spreadsheet you set it up in. It reads the tab and columns you choose (date, name, days before, recipients) to find reminders that are due. It does not change your cells.
  • Sending email as you. Reminder emails are sent from your own Google account to the addresses you list.
  • Running when you're not present. A daily timer lets reminders go out while the sheet is closed.
  • Your email address. Used only to pre-fill "Send reminders to" and to tell you if a reminder check fails.
  • A sidebar. Shows the settings screen inside Google Sheets.

How the data is used

The data above is used only to work out which reminders are due and to send them. It is not used for anything else: no advertising, no profiling, no analytics and no building of datasets. We (the developer) never see it.

What is shared, and with whom

The only data that leaves the spreadsheet is the reminder email itself. It contains the item name and date from the row that is due, and it is sent from your own Gmail account only to the recipients you entered in the add-on or listed in a column you chose. Nothing is sent to us or to any other person, company or service. We do not sell, rent or transfer Google user data to anyone, including advertisers and data brokers.

How your data is protected

The add-on has no servers, no database and no outside connections, so your data never leaves Google's own systems. It runs on Google Apps Script under your account, protected by Google's security and by the sharing settings of your spreadsheet. Only the permissions listed above are requested, and they are used only for the purposes listed above.

Retention and deletion

Your settings and a short log of reminders already sent (so the same reminder is not sent twice) are saved in the spreadsheet's own document properties, inside your Google account. We keep no copy anywhere. They stay there until you delete them: "Extensions > Gridoo Reminders > Turn off reminders" stops all processing and removes the daily timer, uninstalling the add-on stops all access, and deleting the spreadsheet deletes the stored settings and log with it. Reminder emails already sent stay in your Sent folder and the recipients' inboxes like any other email. You can also revoke the add-on's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google user data

Gridoo Reminders' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data to develop, improve or train general artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Payments

Gridoo Reminders is free and takes no payments. The add-on does not connect to any outside service. If we add a paid plan later, we will update this policy before it launches and explain exactly what is shared.

This website and the waitlist

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics. If you join the waitlist, the answers you give in our Google Form are stored in our Google account and used only to tell you about Gridoo add-ons. To be removed, email us and we will delete your entry.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing gridoo.apps@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell users inside the add-on.
